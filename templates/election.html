Fair election rules
These are enforced by the database itself, not just this screen.
  • Votes can never be edited or deleted.
  • Each voter can vote only once per election.
  • Ballots are secret — they don't record who voted.
  • Results stay sealed until voting closes.
  • A closed election can never be reopened.
  • Every ballot is hash-chained, so tampering is detectable.
